CLEAR ANSWERS

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is included in the complimentary 7-Day Trial Pass?

Your 7-Day Pass provides full access to our Iron Arena, Cardio Loft, all group fitness classes (Boxing, HIIT, Vinyasa), the Olympic saline lap pool, and one complimentary InBody 770 body composition assessment. No payment required upfront.

Can I pause or freeze my membership while traveling?

Yes. Members can freeze their account for up to 90 consecutive days per calendar year directly through the member portal with zero penalty fees.

Are locker rooms, saunas, and towel service complimentary?

All members receive access to our digital locker suites, rain showers, and dry steam rooms. The thermal cold plunges, infrared saunas, and organic Malin+Goetz towel service are included with Signature and Sovereign tiers.

Do I need to make reservations for group classes?

To preserve low coach-to-athlete ratios, class bookings open 48 hours in advance through our member portal or front desk kiosk.
