# 🏋️ KINETIX – Luxury Athletic Club Website

**Redefine Your Peak. Sculpt Your Strongest Self.**

KINETIX is a modern, premium fitness club website designed to deliver a luxury digital experience. It showcases advanced fitness programs, world-class facilities, professional coaching, membership plans, and a membership registration experience.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://lustrous-tulumba-7e4ba2.netlify.app/

---

## ✨ Features

- **Premium Homepage:** Modern and attractive landing page with a luxury fitness theme.
- **Fitness Programs:** Explore strength training, HIIT, boxing, and mobility programs.
- **Luxury Facilities:** Showcase of gym equipment, swimming pool, sauna, recovery areas, and nutrition bar.
- **Expert Coaches:** Dedicated section introducing professional fitness coaches.
- **Membership Plans:** Three membership options with different pricing and benefits.
- **FAQ Section:** Answers to frequently asked questions.
- **Membership Registration:** Form for visitors to submit their membership details.
- **Responsive Design:** Designed to provide a smooth browsing experience across different screen sizes.
- **Modern UI:** Premium typography, attractive visuals, and elegant layouts.
